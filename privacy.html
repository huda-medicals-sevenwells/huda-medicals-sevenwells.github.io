Privacy Policy

Huda Medicals, Seven Wells, Chennai · Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: this website has no server and no customer accounts. Your lists, addresses and past orders stay on your own phone. We only see what you send us on WhatsApp. If you use Google backup, the copy is kept in your own Google Drive, and we cannot see it.

Who we are

Huda Medicals, 109(52), St. Xavier Street, Seven Wells, George Town, Chennai 600001. Retail drug licences TN/Z01/20/00923 and TN/Z01/21/00923. Email: hudamedicals.sevenwells@gmail.com. Phone: 99622 69992.

What we receive

Only what you choose to send us on WhatsApp: the medicines you ask for, any note you type, and, if you added them, your name, phone number and delivery address or map location. Prescriptions and photos are sent by you inside WhatsApp. We use this only to check availability, prepare your order and deliver it.

What stays on your phone

To save you typing, this website keeps some things in your phone's browser storage:

  • your cart, Buy later list and family lists
  • your name, phone number and saved addresses
  • your past orders (to offer Reorder and refill reminders)

This stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Location and voice search

Your live location is used only if you allow it, only while the page is open, and is sent to us only as a map link in your WhatsApp message. Voice search uses your phone's own speech feature; we do not receive the recording.

Google backup (optional)

If you tap Back up with Google, you sign in with your Google account and a copy of the items listed above is saved in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this website can use. This website cannot see or change any of your other Drive files, emails or contacts. Huda Medicals has no access to your backup. You can delete it any time: in Google Drive on a computer, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Huda Medicals and choose Delete hidden app data. To remove this website's access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics tools on this website.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Services we use

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your messages to us travel through WhatsApp. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete the order details we hold from your WhatsApp messages, contact us at the email or phone above.
